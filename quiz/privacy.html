Privacy notice

Wick is a quiz on this website. Anyone can play it, including children. This notice is in plain English. It is not a legal essay.

What Wick does not do

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or other trackers.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not load scripts, fonts, or images from other websites.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, age, or any other personal data.
  • It does not send your answers or your score to a server.

What stays on this device

Wick uses the browser’s local storage for two things only:

  • your best score
  • whether sound is on or off

That storage is strictly necessary for those features, which are part of the game you choose to play. Nothing in it is sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

Children

The questions are written to be family-friendly. Wick does not collect personal data from children or from anyone else.
