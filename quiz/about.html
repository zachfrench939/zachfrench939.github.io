Wick

About Wick and terms

Wick is a free daily general knowledge quiz. Each UK day, from midnight to midnight in London, everyone gets the same 100 questions. You have 1 minute, and one scored attempt. After that you can practise the same questions. Practice is not scored.

A candle burns down beside the questions, in step with the 60-second clock. The round ends when the flame goes out, you finish the 100, or you tap End game.

It works as ordinary files in the browser. It does not need an account, a build step, or a connection to another website after the page has loaded. A later version may add an optional shared score list. This one does not send scores anywhere.

How a question works

Each question has four answers. Tap one, or press 1, 2, 3, or 4. A right answer moves on at once. A wrong answer shows the right one and locks the buttons for 2 seconds, while the 60-second clock and the candle keep burning. That pause applies in the daily game and in practice.

Scoring

  • First correct answer in a row: 100 points, marked GOT IT.
  • Second in a row: 150, NICE.
  • Third: 200, SHARP.
  • Fourth and beyond: 250, BLAZING.

A wrong answer scores nothing and the answer streak returns to zero. Your best score, today's result, and a streak of days played in a row are kept on this device. The day number in a shared result, Wick #N, counts from 3 November 2026.

A few questions use a precise word, such as “official capital” or “by surface area”, because the short version would have two fair answers.

Sound

Sound is off until you turn it on. The clicks are made in the browser. No sound file is downloaded. The on or off choice is saved on this device.

Sharing

Share result copies a short text summary, with a grid of squares, to your clipboard. Wick does not post it anywhere for you.

Terms

Wick is for fun. Scores have no cash value and are not a certificate. The questions are original to this game and were chosen because the facts stay put, not because they are in the news. They are checked with care. Even so, the quiz is offered as it is, and a wording may not suit every reader.

Please keep it kind. The questions are meant to be family-friendly.
